TPPM PHOTOGRAMS

What is a photogram?

Workshop participants share the magical process of creating images without a camera and create photographs of compositions made of different objects. The process, first of all, involves assembling the composition and then revealing it in a dark room.

Over the course of two months, up to 25 local participants in the Pankisi Gorge delved into the power of visual storytelling, learning practical tools to inspire positive transformation in their communities.
