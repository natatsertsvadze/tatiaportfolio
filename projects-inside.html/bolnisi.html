BOLNISI

The “Visual Storytelling Camp”

Participants explored the history of photography and experimented with creating images without using a camera. The camp aims to encourage positive change among 12–17-year-old youth in Bolnisi through the use of visual arts (photography and video).

The educational course is implemented as part of the project “Visual Education School: An Alternative Tool for Civic Development.” The project is carried out by the Photography & Multimedia Center, in partnership with the Tbilisi Photo Festival, with financial support from the Embassy of the Kingdom of the Netherlands in Georgia. The local partner of the project is the Bolnisi Adult Education Center.
